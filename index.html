Health & wellness

Simple tips for everyday wellbeing.

Educational information only. It is not a diagnosis or a replacement for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

🧼
Prevention

Make hand hygiene part of your routine

Wash hands with soap and running water, especially before eating and after using the toilet. If hands are visibly dirty, soap and water are preferred.

🩹
First aid

Be prepared for minor everyday injuries

Keep basic first-aid supplies accessible, check expiry dates, and seek professional care for serious injuries or signs of infection.

💊
Medicine safety

Use medicines safely

Follow the label and pharmacist's instructions, do not share prescription medicines, and ask a qualified professional before combining medicines or supplements.

Public health update · 27 October 2026

Ebola: stay informed, not alarmed.

Use official health sources for updates. This section should be reviewed regularly by the pharmacy before publication or whenever guidance changes.

What is confirmed?

As of the latest official reports available for this page, Nigeria has confirmed one imported case of Bundibugyo Ebola disease in the Federal Capital Territory. NCDC's 24 October situation report said a suspected case reported in Rivers State tested negative. This does not mean people should ignore official public-health advice; the situation can change.

How it spreads: Ebola spreads through direct contact with blood or other body fluids of a person who is ill with or has died from Ebola, or contaminated objects. It is not spread simply by being near someone in ordinary day-to-day contact without exposure to infectious fluids.

Ways to reduce risk

  • Wash hands often with soap and water or use an appropriate alcohol-based hand rub when hands are not visibly dirty.
  • Avoid direct contact with blood and body fluids, and do not handle items contaminated by a suspected case.
  • Do not touch or prepare the body of someone who may have died from Ebola; leave this to trained public-health teams.
  • Do not self-diagnose or spread unverified rumours. Follow NCDC and state health authority guidance.
  • Healthcare workers should use recommended infection-prevention procedures and protective equipment.

If you feel unwell or may have been exposed

Symptoms can include fever, weakness, headache, muscle pain, sore throat, vomiting or diarrhoea, but these symptoms can also have other causes. If you believe you had a relevant exposure or develop concerning symptoms, avoid close contact with others and call public-health authorities or a healthcare facility for instructions before arriving, so they can guide you safely.

Do not rely on a WhatsApp pharmacy chat to assess a possible Ebola exposure. Contact public-health professionals promptly.

About E-Drug Planet

Serving Rivers State since 2016.

Home of genuine drugs.

Established in 2016, E-Drug Planet Pharmacy was founded with a clear purpose: to help people access genuine medications they can trust. We believe everyone deserves reliable information and quality pharmacy care when making decisions about their health.

Our mission is to help bridge the gap between substandard medicines and dependable healthcare by prioritising genuine, quality-assured products and responsible service. We aim to serve the public with medicines that meet appropriate quality standards, while encouraging customers to seek guidance from a qualified pharmacist and use medicines as directed.

Based in Oyigbo, with two branches serving customers across Rivers State, we remain committed to making trusted pharmacy support more accessible. Product availability, current prices and branch details should be confirmed with our team before an order is finalised.

Our promise: genuine-care service, helpful guidance, and a commitment to putting customers’ wellbeing first.
